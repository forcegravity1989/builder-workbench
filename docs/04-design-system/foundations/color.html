Usage · 用法

The chrome stays monochrome — grounds, hairlines and ink tints (color-mix of --color-text) — and saturation is reserved for the primary action, links, focus and status. Light ramp steps (100–300) make tints and hovers; 500 is the base; 700–900 carry text on tints and pressed states. On the Claude ground, accent-colored body text takes --color-accent-700.

界面骨架保持单色 — 底色、发丝线与墨色叠加(color-mix)承担结构;饱和色只出现在主操作、链接、焦点与状态上。色阶浅段(100–300)做浅底与悬停,500 为基色,700–900 承担浅底上的文字与按压态。Claude 底色上的强调正文用 --color-accent-700。
