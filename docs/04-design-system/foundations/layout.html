Rules · 规则

Everything sits on multiples of 4; rows are 28px (sidebar), 36px (lists, ⌘K). Structure is drawn with --color-divider hairlines, not boxes — three elevation levels only (hairline, --shadow-sm, floating --shadow-lg). Labels align left; numbers, counts and shortcuts align right. Don't nest cards.

一切落在 4 的倍数上;行高 28px(侧边栏)、36px(列表与 ⌘K)。结构用 --color-divider 发丝线画,不用盒子 — 层级只有三档:发丝线、--shadow-sm、浮层 --shadow-lg。文字左对齐,数字、计数与快捷键右对齐。卡片不嵌套。
